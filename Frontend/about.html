About

What this application does and how it works.

PUCIT GPA Assistant helps PUCIT BS(CS) students work with their GPA and CGPA: converting marks to grade points, calculating a semester GPA, projecting a CGPA, and finding the GPA needed to reach a target.

All calculations are performed by the backend, using the official PUCIT grading scale and scheme of studies. This interface only collects your input and displays the result — it does not calculate GPA or CGPA itself.

Good to know

Quran Translation courses carry 0.5 credit hours each. Math Deficiency courses (MD-001, MD-002) are non-credit, pass/fail, and are excluded from GPA and CGPA calculations.
